# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Frogify is an open-source project, and everyone is welcome to participate.

We are committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone who interacts with the project, regardless of experience level, background, identity, nationality, appearance, disability, or personal beliefs.

The goal is simple: keep discussions productive, technical, and respectful.

## Expected Behavior

When participating in Frogify, please:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give technical feedback without attacking the person behind the contribution.
- Assume good faith when possible.
- Accept reasonable criticism and corrections.
- Respect differences in experience, knowledge, language, and perspective.
- Keep issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews relevant to the project.
- Help make the project welcoming to new contributors.
- Respect maintainers' decisions regarding project scope and technical direction.

Disagreement is completely acceptable. Harassment and personal attacks are not.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Personal insults or hostile comments.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Threats or encouragement of violence.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Repeated unwanted contact.
- Spam or excessive self-promotion unrelated to Frogify.
- Deliberately derailing technical discussions.
- Abusing issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project communication channels.

## Technical Discussions

Frogify welcomes criticism of code, architecture, documentation, features, and technical decisions.

Comments such as:

> "This implementation can cause a race condition."

are appropriate.

Comments attacking the contributor instead of the contribution are not.

Focus criticism on the code or idea, explain the reasoning, and propose an improvement when possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Frogify project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues
- pull requests
- code reviews
- discussions
- documentation contributions
- project-related communication
- other public spaces where someone is representing Frogify

## Reporting Problems

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the project maintainer.

**Do not open a public GitHub issue to report harassment, abuse, or other sensitive conduct matters.**

You can contact the maintainer through the contact information available on the GitHub profile:

https://github.com/wittg3n

Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as reasonably possible.

When reporting an incident, include:

- what happened;
- where it happened;
- relevant links or screenshots;
- who was involved;
- any additional context that may help evaluate the situation.

False or deliberately misleading reports may themselves be considered a violation of this policy.

## Enforcement

Depending on the severity and circumstances, maintainers may take actions including:

1. Providing a private warning.
2. Removing or editing inappropriate comments or contributions.
3. Temporarily restricting participation.
4. Rejecting or closing issues or pull requests.
5. Permanently banning someone from participating in the project.
6. Reporting serious abuse to GitHub or the relevant platform.

Enforcement decisions are made with the goal of protecting the project and its community.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- applying this policy consistently;
- handling reports fairly;
- protecting reporter privacy where reasonably possible;
- avoiding retaliation against people who make good-faith reports;
- taking proportionate action when violations occur.

## Good-Faith Participation

Mistakes happen.

A minor misunderstanding, disagreement, or poorly worded message will normally be handled differently from repeated harassment, threats, discrimination, or deliberate abuse.

Participants are encouraged to correct mistakes, apologize where appropriate, and move discussions back toward productive collaboration.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is informed by commonly used open-source community standards, including the Contributor Covenant, and has been adapted for the Frogify project.
